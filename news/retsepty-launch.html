Meet Retsepty: a recipe book that fills itself

You saved a hundred recipe videos and still can’t find the one you need. Retsepty turns a link, a screenshot or a photo of a cookbook page into a proper recipe, ready to cook.

TikTok linkCookbook photo
Creamy mushroom pasta
  • 400 g pasta
  • 300 g mushrooms
  • 200 ml cream
4 servings · 610 kcal

How it works

  1. Send it

    Share a TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube video straight from the app, paste a website link, or photograph up to six cookbook pages.

  2. It becomes a recipe

    Retsepty pulls out the ingredients, the steps and calories with protein, fat and carbs per serving.

  3. Cook

    Open cooking mode and follow big, readable steps with timers.

What’s inside

Import from anywhere

Links, the Share button in TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, photos or plain text.

Servings that recalculate

Change the number of servings and every amount follows. Nutrition is shown per serving.

A smart shopping list

The same product from different recipes is added up into one line. Salt and water stay off the list.

Cooking mode

Large steps, several timers running at once, and the screen stays on while you cook.

One book for the family

Share your recipes and shopping list with the people you cook for using a six-digit code.

Find it fast

Search by dish name or by what’s in your fridge, and keep favourites a tap away.

Available now

Retsepty is available for Android in early access: sign-up works with an invitation code. An iOS version will follow.
