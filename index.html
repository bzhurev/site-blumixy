What we build

Three things, done properly. Every project starts small and earns its next step.

AI & automation

Assistants and agents that do real work inside your tools, not demos that stay demos.

  • Chat assistants and agents wired into your systems
  • Retrieval over your own documents (RAG)
  • Workflow automation: from prototype to pilot to production

Software delivery

Web and mobile products with a lean stack and a short path from idea to release.

  • Web and mobile apps, from MVP to v1
  • Internal tools and dashboards
  • Data pipelines and integrations between services

UX/UI & release

Interfaces people understand on the first try, shipped with monitoring in place.

  • Rapid prototyping and usability reviews
  • Release, observability and iteration after launch

Games

Cozy casual titles with clean UI and satisfying mechanics. Our first one is almost out.

  • Blumixy Puzzle, a merge-and-match for iOS and Android
  • Devlogs and playtests in the open

How a project runs

Short cycles with a measurable result at the end of each one.

  1. Scope

    One call to agree what success looks like and what we leave out.

  2. Prototype

    A working slice in days, tested on real data or real users.

  3. Pilot

    Running with a small group, measured against the agreed goal.

  4. Production

    Hardened, monitored and handed over, or run by us.
