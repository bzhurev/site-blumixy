About blumixy

We are a small product studio. We design and ship AI-powered software and pragmatic integrations for other teams, and we make our own casual games.

What we believe

Clarity over noise

If an interface or a report needs explaining, it isn’t finished yet.

Pragmatic engineering

The simplest stack that does the job, with room to grow when it’s needed.

Outcomes over output

We measure success by what changed for you, not by hours or features shipped.

How we work

  • Short cycles with a measurable milestone at the end of each one.
  • A lean stack with logging and monitoring from day one.
  • Care for performance and UX in everything we ship, from internal tools to games.
